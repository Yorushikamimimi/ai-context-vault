# Idea and Deferred-Item Backlog

Use this when good ideas keep coming up in conversations and get lost once the chat ends. Copy the table into a backlog file of your own (for example `50-Workflow/ideas.md`).

## What goes in

- New ideas the owner raised or explicitly agreed with, and things the owner said to "do later". The agent's own suggestions do not go in unless the owner endorses them.
- Not: to-dos that already belong to a project (write them in that project's current-progress document), or dated life tasks (use a reminder app).

## Recording

The conversation that heard the idea adds one row when it closes out:

| Recorded | Idea | Why it seemed interesting | First step, if done | Source | Times deferred |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `{{YYYY-MM-DD}}` | `{{IDEA}}` | `{{WHY}}` | `{{FIRST_STEP}}` | `{{CONVERSATION_OR_TOOL}}` | 0 |

## Weekly review

- The first time each week the owner talks to the coordinating session, if the last review is more than seven days old and rows are waiting, ask once: "There are N items in the backlog; want to go through them?" If the owner says "later", do not ask again that week. No scheduled jobs or unprompted messages.
- For each row ask only: continue, drop, or defer. Drop deletes the row without explanation. Continue moves it to the right project or area and starts from its "why" and "first step". Defer adds one; at two, suggest dropping it next time.
- Update the last-review date and add one line to a review record.
